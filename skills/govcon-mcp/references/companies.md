# Companies and people

Resolve identity before combining evidence. Preserve legal name, DBA, UEI, returned website, source IDs and corporate-family distinctions.

For a UEI, use an exact supported identifier lookup; a malformed identifier must not silently become a name search. For a website, normalize the domain and use a capability that matches a recorded domain if exposed. A search using the brand token is candidate generation only. Do not report a confirmed domain match unless the returned evidence establishes it. For a name, `g2x_search_companies` can return multiple legal entities; preserve disambiguation and ask which one when necessary.

Use contract history, socioeconomic status, vehicle usage, public graph links and evidence tools for an incumbent or teaming brief. A corporate family is not one award recipient; shared NAICS, a similar name or a graph neighbor does not prove a teaming relationship. State what supports the suggested partner and what remains unknown.

Distinguish discoverable G2X members (`g2x_people_directory`) from professional profiles (`g2x_people_profiles`). Directory visibility follows account permissions. Resolve the person against employer, role and available identifiers; do not merge namesakes. Do not infer private contact details.

Company signals and web profiles can supply news/social evidence and can consume paid allowances. Use them when relevant to the question and within authorized usage. Cite the original returned evidence and its publication date where available. Distinguish company statements from independent reporting, current employment from historical employment, and inference from fact.

Return a resolved identity or explicit candidates, a concise brief, citations and the decision the evidence supports. Research does not authorize adding contacts to CRM or sending outreach.
